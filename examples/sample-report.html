What this report does and doesn't prove

It shows:

  • Every entry was signed by whoever holds the private key that matches the public key used for this check (fingerprint ab91c309743610d9).
  • Unless the verdict says otherwise, no entry was edited, inserted, removed from the middle or reordered after it was written.
  • If checkpoints were supplied, the log still holds everything up to the latest checkpoint.

It does not show:

  • That the agent recorded everything it did. Actions taken outside the recorded tools do not appear here.
  • That nothing was deleted from the end of the log after the last checkpoint. Without checkpoints, entries removed from the end cannot be detected.
  • That the holder of the private key did not rewrite the log before a checkpoint was taken. Keep the key away from the agent and keep checkpoints where the operator cannot edit them.
  • That the times are correct. They come from the computer's own clock.
  • Who the approvers really are. Names are recorded as the software supplied them.
  • What the inputs and outputs were, unless full capture was switched on. By default only fingerprints (hashes) are kept, which can confirm a value you already have but not reveal it.
